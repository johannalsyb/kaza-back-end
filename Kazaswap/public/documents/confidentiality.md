At Kazaswap.co, we highly value and prioritize the privacy and confidentiality of our users. This User Confidentiality Page outlines the measures and policies we have put in place to ensure that your personal information remains secure when using our website.

When you register as a user on Kazaswap.co, we may collect certain personal information from you. We assure you that this information will only be used for the purposes stated in our Privacy Policy.

### Storage and Protection of Personal Information:

All personal information collected from users is stored securely on our servers. We have implemented industry-standard security measures to protect this data from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. Our team continuously monitors and updates these security protocols to maintain the highest level of protection for your information.

### ‍Use of Personal Information:

We will only use your personal information for the following purposes:

- To improve and enhance our website and services.

- To comply with legal and regulatory requirements.

### Sharing of Personal Information:

We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to any third parties for their marketing purposes. Your personal information will only be shared in the following circumstances:
- With your consent, for any purpose not listed in this User Confidentiality Page.
- To comply with legal obligations, enforce our Terms of Service, or protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property, as well as that of our users and the public.

Kazaswap.co may use cookies or similar tracking technologies to enhance user experience and gather information about how the website is used. These technologies help us understand user preferences, tailor content, and improve our services. You have the option to manage your cookie preferences through your browser settings.

As a user of Kazaswap.co, it is your responsibility to maintain the confidentiality of your login credentials and account information. Please do not share this information with others, and ensure that you log out of your account when using shared devices. If you suspect any unauthorized access to your account, please notify us immediately.
